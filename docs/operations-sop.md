# Operations SOP: Request to Closure

## Objective
Ensure every maintenance request is handled consistently, on time, and with clear accountability.

## Roles
- **Company Owner**: policy and escalation authority.
- **Maintenance Owner**: operational controller.
- **Maintenance Staff**: field execution.
- **Customer**: requestor and service confirmer.

## SOP steps
1. **Request Intake**
   - Customer submits reason, address, location, preferred time, and payment method.
2. **Validation**
   - Confirm service category, address completeness, area serviceability, and urgency.
3. **Assignment**
   - Offer task to eligible area staff by availability and skills.
4. **Acceptance Window**
   - Staff must accept/reject within a fixed response time.
   - If expired, auto-reassign to next eligible staff.
5. **Travel and Check-In**
   - Staff navigates with map support and checks in on arrival.
6. **Execution**
   - Staff performs maintenance and records work notes/photos.
7. **Completion and Check-Out**
   - Staff marks completion with proof and closure comments.
8. **Payment**
   - Capture payment via card/wallet/cash and record transaction status/reference.
9. **Customer Confirmation**
   - Confirm service completion and collect rating/feedback.
10. **Closure**
   - Close task only after service completion and payment state update.

## Escalation matrix
- **L1:** Auto-alert for delayed acceptance or overdue in-progress tasks.
- **L2:** Maintenance Owner intervention for unresolved delays/failures.
- **L3:** Company Owner escalation for SLA or repeated incident breaches.

## Operational checklists
### Daily
- Unassigned tasks
- Overdue tasks
- Payment failures
- Repeat complaints

### Weekly
- KPI review
- Staff performance and workload balancing
- Incident root-cause review
- Backlog and roadmap updates

## Records to maintain
- Task lifecycle log
- Payment transaction log
- Incident log
- Decision/change log
